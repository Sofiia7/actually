## 1. What the Extension does

Actually shows market-implied probabilities sourced from Polymarket for news articles you choose to check ("Discovery"). If you connect a Web3 wallet via WalletConnect, you may also place orders on Polymarket directly through the Extension ("Trading").

**Probabilities shown are not predictions, advice, or guarantees.** They reflect the aggregate trading positions of Polymarket users at the time the data was fetched and may be stale. Actually is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Polymarket, OpenAI, Cloudflare, WalletConnect / Reown, or any news publisher.

The order price, size, and outcome are unaffected by this attribution - you trade at the same prices you would on Polymarket directly.

The builder code in use is shown in **Settings → About**. We reserve the right to change it across updates.
